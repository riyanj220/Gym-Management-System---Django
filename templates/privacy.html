Data Governance

Privacy & Data Protection

Committed to protecting your personal information and privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential information to manage your membership, customize workout programming, and maintain secure portal access:

  • Account identifiers (username, email address, password hash)
  • Contact numbers and billing preferences
  • Fitness assessment details and coach messaging logs
2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to deliver personalized fitness experiences, facilitate communication with assigned coaches, process membership renewals, and notify you of facility schedules.

3. Data Security & Payment Processing

All sensitive financial transactions are routed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Stripe). We do not store raw card numbers on our local databases.

4. Your Control & Access Rights

You have full authority to update your profile data, request a copy of stored records, or close your profile by navigating to your Account Settings.

Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding your data, contact our compliance team at privacy@rjgym.com.
